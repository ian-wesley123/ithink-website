OUR SCOPE

Get ready to learn Information systems.

An information system is a set of people, processes, data, and technology that work together to collect, process, store, and provide information to support an organization's operations and decision-making.

Information systems on daily transactions and their challenges

Examine how information systems support business operations, financial activities, reporting, decision-making, and collaboration among different departments, as well as their benefits, potential impacts of system downtime, and common challenges encountered by users.

01

Integrated Efficiency

The system is mainly used for internal purposes, while customers can access a shared point for certain services. It integrates payroll, inventory, and accounting through the ERP system and supports invoice generation for outsourcing transactions. The system improves efficiency by reducing manpower and manual input, with a backup system in Singapore to support continuous operations if the main server in Denmark encounters problems. A common challenge is when customers request reports that are not available in the system, which is addressed by creating customized reports and summarizing the required information.

02

Operational Efficiency

The organization uses distinct information systems for daily transactions, managerial reporting, and executive decision-making, while integrated systems allow departments to share information. These systems improve efficiency by automating tasks, reducing manpower, and saving time compared with manual processes. If the system becomes unavailable, operations may stop, resulting in revenue loss, backlogs, overtime, and heavier workloads. Common challenges include slow system performance and inefficient user interfaces, which are addressed through system maintenance, hardware upgrades, and automation of repetitive tasks.

03

Integrated Management

The CRM system supports the company's daily operations by monitoring attendance, employee performance, orders, deliveries, and inventory. It serves as an all-in-one platform that allows different processes to be monitored in one place, making operations more efficient. The system is important because errors can disrupt daily operations and make administrative tasks more difficult. The main challenges are internet connectivity and employee adaptation, which the company addresses by improving the connection and guiding employees who have difficulty using the technology.
